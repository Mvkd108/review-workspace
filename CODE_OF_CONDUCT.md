# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps this project:

- Giving and accepting technical criticism gracefully.
- Being welcoming to people with less context than you.
- Saying "I was wrong" when you were.
- Focusing disagreement on the work.

Behaviour that does not belong here:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any space where you
are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this document,
and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations.
